## Final generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: full-bleed desktop website hero background, panoramic landscape 1536x1024. Create a spectacular One Piece inspired anime pirate adventure seascape: a small elegant pirate sailing ship viewed from behind in the lower right quadrant, brilliant teal ocean currents, towering fantastical jungle islands and limestone cliffs at right and far horizon, warm golden sunrise breaking through dramatic painterly clouds, tiny soaring seabirds. Rich hand-painted anime movie background, ink outlines, exquisite ocean texture, dreamy adventurous scale. Left half should be dark navy open ocean and atmospheric clouds with quiet negative space for large cream website headline overlay. Palette deep midnight blue, turquoise and warm amber. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. This is environmental artwork, no characters or portraits.
