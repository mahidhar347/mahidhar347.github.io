# Realistic seascape artwork — v1

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. These are generated photorealistic
artworks, not documentary photographs or official One Piece assets. The earlier
illustration remains untouched. PNG masters and optimized JPEGs are stored locally
as `seascape-real-{sunset,dawn,day,midnight}-v1.*`. Only JPEGs are published.

## Sunset base — final prompt

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: landscape full-bleed website hero background.
Input image 1 is the edit target and composition reference. Re-create this exact
seascape as a photorealistic live-action adventure film still, not anime or an
illustration. Preserve the wide landscape framing, unobstructed left half of open
ocean and sky for website copy, a single detailed wooden sailing ship seen from
behind in the lower right, right-side lush limestone sea cliffs and natural arch,
distant islands and a thin waterfall. Preserve approximate placement, scale, ship
identity and camera angle. Change only the rendering style to physical realism:
real salt-weathered timber, woven ivory canvas with seams, fine rigging, naturally
translucent deep blue-green waves with irregular foam and sun reflections, craggy
eroded limestone, individual tropical foliage, soft atmospheric distance. Natural
late-afternoon golden sunlight from the right with physically consistent shadows,
moderate photographic contrast, restrained natural colors. 35mm cinematic location
photography; believable scale and materials. NOT a color grade of the input:
reconstruct all surfaces, sky, ocean and lighting with photographic detail. No
oversaturation, no magenta tint, no cartoon outlines, no low-poly geometry, no
game-render look, no text, no watermark. Output a single landscape image.

## Relighting variants — final prompts

Each variant uses the sunset output as its edit target. The exact prompts for each
are recorded below as shared instructions plus their respective lighting paragraph.

[Lighting paragraph.] Preserve exact camera, image dimensions and wide framing,
all island and arch geometry, waterfall location, ship design and lower-right

Dawn: Early morning just before sunrise, sun below the horizon, soft pale neutral
rose and blue sky, thin low mist between islands, softly lit cool ocean and gentle

Day: Clear late-morning daylight: sun high overhead outside the frame, blue sky
with sparse natural white cumulus clouds, clear turquoise water in the shallows
and deep blue offshore, bright ivory canvas, crisp neutral sunlight and short
physically correct shadows. Remove the low sunset sun and gold horizon.

Midnight: A genuine moonlit night, sun completely absent, deep dark sky with a few
